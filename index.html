About this project

From race records to racing insights.

This project uses Tableau visualizations to explore Formula 1 data across drivers, teams, race outcomes, tyre strategies, circuits, countries and podium finishes. The dashboard supports broad exploration, while the driver leaderboard story gives one question a closer look: how do the leading drivers compare on points, and how does changing Top N affect the view?
